What we do

Behind every episode

From first interview to final edit, here's what goes into the show — and what you get out of listening.

INTERVIEWS

We meet minds

We sit down with founders, researchers, and alumni who've built something worth talking about, and ask what everyone's actually curious about.

KNOWLEDGE

We grow what we know

Deep-dive segments on science, tech, and ideas that matter — no background required, just curiosity.

STORIES

Real people, real stories

Every guest has a story worth telling. We let them tell it — unscripted, no filter.

PRODUCTION

Behind the mic

Recording, editing, hosting, design — every episode is built by a student crew learning the craft as they go.

EPISODES

New episodes, every season

Short conversations, long ones, solo segments — see what's coming up on the Upcoming Eps page.

COMMUNITY

Open to every student

No experience needed to join the crew, suggest a guest, or just come watch a recording — just show up curious.
